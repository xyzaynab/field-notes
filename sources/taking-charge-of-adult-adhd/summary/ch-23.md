# Chapter 23. Rule 8: Have a Sense of Humor!
Pages 194–196. (Ends Step Four.)

1. **Main idea.** Rule 8 helps "accept your imperfections and get on with your life" (p.194): "ADHD may be serious, but you don't always have to be" (p.194). Owning mistakes with humor preserves self-esteem and relationships (p.195).
2. **Named method/routine.** Rule 8 script: say with a smile, "Well, there goes my ADHD talking [or acting up] again. Sorry about that. My mistake. Now I have to try to do something about that next time." (p.194) This does four things: owns the mistake; explains how it may have happened; apologizes without blaming others; promises to try to do better (pp.194–195). Time: not stated. Also: a private laugh at yourself is "almost always appropriate" (p.195).
3. **Tools/containers.** **Eight-rules page**: photocopy and tape to bathroom mirror; keep a copy on desk at work, in day planner, beside computer monitor, and in other places you look repeatedly (p.196). Job: daily re-exposure until memorized (p.196). Barkley did this with Covey's *7 Habits* (p.196). Humor resources: Dani Donovan's artwork; videos by Jessica McCabe and Rick Green (p.196).
4. **Notes/thoughts/reference.** Not addressed.
5. **Transfers/review.** Seeing the rules "every day" (p.196). No review step.
6. **Worksheets/deadlines.** The one-page summary of the eight rules (p.196):
- Rule 1 Stop the Action!—buy some time before you respond.
- Rule 2 See the Past … and Then the Future—see what's coming.
- Rule 3 Say the Past … and Then the Future—analyze before deciding; develop rules for the future.
- Rule 4 Externalize Key Information—rely on something besides your memory.
- Rule 5 Feel the Future—stay motivated.
- Rule 6 Break It Down … and Make It Matter—bring the future a lot closer.
- Rule 7 Make Problems External, Physical, and Manual—simplify problem solving.
- Rule 8 Have a Sense of Humor!—accept your imperfections and get on with your life.
7. **Key terms.** *Social toast*: result of denying or excusing your ADHD behavior (p.195). *Know it, own it, work with it* (p.195, echoing Step Two).
