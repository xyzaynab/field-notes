# Chapter 14. The Nonstimulants
Pages 137–144.

1. **Main idea.** Nonstimulants (chiefly atomoxetine/Strattera and guanfacine XR/Intuniv) are alternatives with little or no abuse potential, slower dose adjustment, and somewhat less improvement than stimulants for some people (pp.137–143). About 75% respond to either type; ~50% respond to both, ~25% better on a stimulant, ~25% better on atomoxetine (p.138). Author's claims; prescribing decisions rest with a doctor.
2. **Named method/routine.** None. Atomoxetine: once or twice daily, mostly once; dose adjustment takes 2–4 weeks (pp.138). Guanfacine XR: swallow whole; dose adjustment slow; "often viewed as a third-line medicine" (p.143). Bupropion (Wellbutrin): off-label, mainly when anxiety/depression coexist (p.143). Modafinil: not approved; no adult research (pp.143–144). "Atomoxetine might be a better choice … if" list (p.141): mild–moderate ADHD; stimulants didn't help; stimulant insomnia; substance abuse history in household; all-day coverage needed; anxiety, depression, tics, obsessions.
3. **Tools/containers.** None.
4. **Notes/thoughts/reference.** Not addressed.
5. **Transfers/review.** Not addressed. Adults starting atomoxetine are told to "be patient" with a few weeks of adjustment (p.138). If drowsy beyond a few weeks, take at night or split the dose (p.138).
6. **Worksheets/deadlines.** None. Nonmedication fallback: Steps Four and Five strategies and ADHD coaching; some clinicians combine low doses of atomoxetine and a stimulant (pp.140–141).
7. **Key terms.**
- *Nonstimulant*: not a controlled substance in the US; little appeal as a drug of abuse (p.139).
- *Off-label use*: using a drug for a condition it isn't FDA-approved for; "can be perfectly safe (and even the best choice)" (p.143).
- *Alpha-2 ports*: guanfacine XR "closes these ports, strengthening signals" along the axon (p.142).
Safety statements as given: liver complications about 1 in a million with atomoxetine (p.139); suicidal-thinking warning on label, not found in teens/adults by the author (p.140); never combine with an MAOI (p.140). Do not open atomoxetine capsules (p.141).
