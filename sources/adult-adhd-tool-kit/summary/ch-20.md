# Ch 20 — Conclusions

Citation convention: EPUB has no page numbers; cites are "Ch 20, ¶ order"; page = n/a. The chapter has no section headings.

## 1. Main idea
ADHD can range from mild to severe and should not be dismissed as "everyone has ADHD." Get an accurate comprehensive assessment, consider medication plus CBT, personalize the plan, and work with a clinician. The point of structure and coping skills is not conformity but "to expand your options for managing your life on your terms" and to decide how you "spend yourself" (Ch 20, ¶¶1–2, 4–9, 11).

None new. Recommended path (prose): confirm ADHD via a comprehensive "gold standard" assessment → treatment plan → medication and/or CBT combined → personalize → work with an ADHD-familiar clinician (Ch 20, ¶¶4–8).
